\section{Executive Summary \& Problem Context}

In India's FMCG wholesale distribution hierarchy, regional distributors bridge the gap between large consumer packaged goods manufacturers (e.g., Parle, ITC, Hindustan Unilever, Britannia) and fragmented retail networks. 

\subsection{Client Profile: Jabalpur FMCG Wholesale Distribution Hub}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Territory:} Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh (covering wholesale clusters including Lordganj, Sarafa, and Ganjipura).
    \item \textbf{Commercial Scale:} $\sim$1,800 active kirana retail stores; 70 total employees; 18 field beat salesmen; ₹85--100 Cr annual turnover.
    \item \textbf{Daily Throughput:} 600 orders per business day across packaged food and home-care product lines.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Core Operational Bottlenecks}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Fragmented, Unstructured Channels:} Over 75\% of daily orders arrive via WhatsApp as colloquial Hinglish text (\textit{"bhaiya 5 peti parle-g chhota aur 2 peti surf excel 1 kilo"}) or photographs of handwritten notebook pages (\textit{parchi}).
    \item \textbf{Manual Tally Entry Bottleneck:} Six full-time office clerks spend 4--5 hours every morning manually transcribing orders, looking up SKU numbers, and typing lines into Tally ERP.
    \item \textbf{Ordering Inaccuracies:} Misidentified pack sizes and out-of-stock items affect $\sim$5\% of all shipments, resulting in return deliveries, inventory discrepancies, and disputed \textit{khata} ledgers.
    \item \textbf{Salesman Network Dead Zones:} Field salesmen taking orders in dense wholesale markets encounter frequent cellular outages, disabling standard cloud-only SaaS applications.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{SupplySetu (सप्लाईसेतु)} replaces these failure points with an automated, role-based platform that unifies retailers, salesmen, and distributor order desk administrators into one synchronized system.

\section{Core Business Logic Flows}

\subsection{Flow 1: Dynamic Tiered Pricing \& Retailer Margin Engine}
Wholesale prices are determined by the retailer's assigned commercial pricing tier:
\begin{equation}
    P_{\text{dealer}} = P_{\text{base}} \times (1 - D_{\text{tier}})
\end{equation}
Retailer profit margin against Maximum Retail Price (MRP) is computed transparently:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Margin \%} = \frac{\text{MRP} - P_{\text{dealer}}}{\text{MRP}} \times 100
\end{equation}

\subsection{Flow 2: Automated Promotional Scheme Engine}
Schemes are evaluated at the line-item level during checkout:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Buy X Get Y Free:} For quantity $Q \ge Q_{\min}$, bonus items are calculated as:
    \begin{equation}
        Q_{\text{free}} = \left\lfloor \frac{Q}{Q_{\min}} \right\rfloor \times Q_{\text{bonus}}
    \end{equation}
    \textit{Example:} Parle-G 10+1 Scheme. Ordering 25 boxes yields $\lfloor 25/10 \rfloor \times 1 = 2$ free boxes.
    \item \textbf{Volume Percentage Discount:} For qualifying bulk quantities, a trade discount is deducted from the line subtotal before GST calculation:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{Discount} = Q \times P_{\text{dealer}} \times \frac{D_{\%}}{100}
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Flow 3: Credit Limit \& Khata Control Circuit}
To prevent bad debts, every incoming order triggers an automated credit line assessment:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Projected Balance} = \text{Outstanding Balance} + \text{Order Final Amount}
\end{equation}
If $\text{Projected Balance} > \text{Credit Limit}$, the order status is flagged with \texttt{CREDIT\_HOLD}. The retailer receives a balance warning, and the order is escalated to the Distributor Admin for manual review and approval.

\subsection{Flow 4: GST Compliance \& Intra-State Tax Split}
For distribution within Madhya Pradesh (State Code 23), invoices comply with Rule 46 of the CGST Rules:
\begin{equation}
    \text{CGST} = \text{SGST} = \frac{\text{Taxable Value} \times \text{GST Rate}}{200}
\end{equation}

\section{How the AI Part Works: Multimodal Parsing \& Safety Guardrails}

The AI ingestion pipeline converts unstructured Hinglish text and photographed paper notebook slips (\textit{parchi}) into verified shopping cart items.

\subsection{Processing Pipeline}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Ingestion:} The user pastes Hinglish text or uploads an image of a handwritten \textit{parchi} slip.
    \item \textbf{Catalogue Grounding:} The request is dispatched to Google Gemini 1.5 Flash alongside the distributor's active product catalogue and schema constraints.
    \item \textbf{Confidence Scoring:} The model extracts product candidates and returns structured JSON with item confidence scores ($0.0 \le c \le 1.0$).
    \item \textbf{Anti-Hallucination Guardrail:} Items with confidence below 70\% or SKU codes not present in the database are filtered out.
    \item \textbf{Human-in-the-Loop Review:} The extracted items are presented in an interactive review modal where the user can adjust quantities, select pack sizes, and confirm prices.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Timeout Fallback \& Deterministic Parsing}
If the Gemini API encounters a network timeout ($>8$ seconds) or rate limit, the system switches to a built-in FMCG regex parser (\texttt{ai.service.ts}). This fallback uses keyword extraction for packaging units (\textit{peti}, \textit{carton}, \textit{kilo}) and fuzzy matching against catalogue SKU names, ensuring that ordering is never blocked by external service outages.

\section{Offline-First Beat Salesman Architecture}

Salesmen taking orders in dense or rural markets rely on a local-first offline architecture powered by Dexie.js (IndexedDB).

\subsection{Offline Order Queuing}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Local Catalog Caching:} Product and retailer lists are cached locally in browser IndexedDB.
    \item \textbf{Idempotency Key Generation:} Each offline order receives a cryptographically generated UUID v4 (\texttt{clientOrderId}) at the time of creation.
    \item \textbf{Transactional Store:} Orders are written to Dexie's \texttt{offlineOrders} store with status \texttt{PENDING\_SYNC}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Automatic Synchronization}
When connectivity is restored, a background listener submits queued orders to \texttt{POST /api/orders/sync}. The server checks the \texttt{clientOrderId} to prevent duplicate insertions, updates local records to \texttt{SYNCED}, and refreshes the salesman's order log.

\section{Scope Boundaries: Cut Scope \& Next Steps}

\subsection{Features Cut During the 7-Day Sprint}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Native Android APK:} Replaced with a responsive Progressive Web App (PWA) with IndexedDB offline support, avoiding app store deployment delays.
    \item \textbf{Direct Tally XML Socket Integration:} Replaced with an RFC-4180 compliant CSV export matching Tally ERP's standard import template, eliminating complex local daemon installations.
    \item \textbf{Instant Payment Gateway:} B2B FMCG operates primarily on rolling 7--15 day credit terms (\textit{khata}). Immediate UPI/card payment was cut in favor of a full \textit{khata} ledger tracking module.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Roadmap for Future Development}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Official WhatsApp Business API Bot:} Ingest orders directly from incoming merchant WhatsApp chats.
    \item \textbf{GeoJSON Beat Route Optimization:} Route sequencing algorithms to minimize salesman travel distance between kiranas.
    \item \textbf{Predictive Kirana Replenishment:} AI-driven demand forecasting to generate proactive reorder suggestions.
    \item \textbf{Automated Tally ODBC Service:} Optional background sync service for direct database-level accounting updates.
\end{itemize}

\section{System Assumptions}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Tax Jurisdiction:} Operations are localized to Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh (State Code 23), with standard 50/50 CGST/SGST splits on intra-state sales.
    \item \textbf{Wholesale Packaging:} Products are sold in commercial packaging units (\texttt{BOX}, \texttt{CARTON}, \texttt{BAG}). Loose individual pieces are not handled.
    \item \textbf{Credit Terms:} Retailers operate on rolling 15-day settlement cycles with credit ceilings enforced prior to order dispatch.
    \item \textbf{Single-Distributor Tenancy:} Designed for a single distributor entity serving multiple retail accounts across assigned geographical beats.
    \item \textbf{Simulated OTP Sign-in:} DLT registration delays are avoided by simulating mobile OTP verification with pre-configured demo credentials.
\end{enumerate}
