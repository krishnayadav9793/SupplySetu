1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

In India's FMCG wholesale distribution landscape, regional distributors connect major consumer packaged goods manufacturers (e.g., Parle, ITC, Hindustan Unilever, Britannia) with thousands of independent neighborhood retail stores (kiranas). The client for this project is a prominent packaged food and home-care wholesale distributor based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, serving approximately 1,800 kirana shops across commercial beats including Lordganj, Sarafa, and Ganjipura. The business employs 70 staff, operates 18 beat salesmen, and generates ≈ ₹85–100 Cr in annual turnover, processing over 600 orders daily.

Despite this scale, the primary ordering pipeline relied entirely on manual, error-prone communication:

  • Unstructured WhatsApp Messages & Handwritten Slips: Kiranas sent colloquial Hinglish text ("bhaiya 5 peti parle-g chhota aur 2 peti surf excel 1 kilo") or photos of handwritten slips (parchi).
  • Manual ERP Entry Bottleneck: Six full-time office staff spent 4–5 hours every morning manually transcribing orders, looking up SKU numbers, and typing invoices into Tally ERP.
  • 5% Order Error Rate: Misidentified pack sizes and out-of-stock items caused frequent delivery returns, inventory discrepancies, and disputed khata ledger balances.
  • Connectivity Blackouts: Beat salesmen visiting congested wholesale clusters encountered frequent cellular dead zones, disabling standard cloud-only web applications.

SupplySetu (सप्लाईसेतु) eliminates these failure points through a unified, multi-role platform featuring multimodal Gemini AI order ingestion, an offline-first Dexie IndexedDB sync engine for beat salesmen, real-time credit-limit controls, and one-click RFC-4180 Tally ERP CSV export.

The relational data model is maintained in PostgreSQL via Prisma ORM v6, enforcing foreign-key cascade policies, unique constraints, and domain enums.

4. Core Business Logic Flows & Formulations

4.1 Flow 1: Dynamic Tiered Pricing & Retailer Margin Calculation

Wholesale distributors offer differentiated dealer rates based on kirana purchase volume and payment history. SupplySetu encodes this into four pricing tiers:

Dealer Rate = Base Price × (1 - Tier Discount Rate)
Retailer Margin % = ((MRP - Dealer Rate) / MRP) × 100
Pricing Tier Discount Applied Example: Parle-G 80g (MRP ₹10.00 / Box Base ₹597.80) Retailer Margin vs MRP
STANDARD 0.0% ₹597.80 / box 17.0%
TIER_1 2.0% ₹585.84 / box 18.6%
TIER_2 4.0% ₹573.89 / box 20.3%
WHOLESALE 6.5% ₹558.94 / box 22.4%

4.2 Flow 2: Automated Promotional Scheme Engine

Schemes are evaluated at line-item aggregation time during checkout:

  • Buy X Get Y Free: If order item quantity Q ≥ Qmin, free bonus units are calculated deterministically:
    Qfree = ⌊ Q / Qmin ⌋ × Qbonus
    Example: Parle-G (Buy 10 Get 1 Free). Ordering 25 boxes automatically adds ⌊ 25/10 ⌋ × 1 = 2 free boxes without inflating the billable line item total.
  • Volume Percentage Discount: If Q ≥ Qmin, an additional trade discount is deducted from the line subtotal before GST computation:
    Discount Amount = Q × Dealer Rate × (Discount % / 100)

4.3 Flow 3: Credit Limit & Khata Control Circuit

To protect wholesale distributors from bad debt, every order passes automated credit verification:

Projected Balance = Outstanding Balance + New Order Final Amount
  • Within Limit: If Projected Balance ≤ Credit Limit, order proceeds to PENDING queue for standard fulfillment.
  • Credit Hold: If Projected Balance > Credit Limit, order is flagged with CREDIT_HOLD. The retailer is notified with their available credit balance, and the Distributor Admin must explicitly approve the credit exception prior to warehouse picking.

4.4 Flow 4: Statutory GST Split (Rule 46 Compliance)

For distribution within Madhya Pradesh (State Code 23), intra-state invoices split GST equally between CGST and SGST:

CGST Amount = SGST Amount = (Taxable Subtotal × GST Rate %) / 200

5. Multimodal AI Order Capture & Safety Guardrails

The AI ingestion pipeline converts unstructured natural-language Hinglish messages and photographed handwritten notebook slips (parchi) into verified, structured cart items.

Core Processing Steps:
  1. Ingestion: The user pastes Hinglish text or uploads an image of a handwritten parchi slip.
  2. Catalogue Grounding: The request is dispatched to Google Gemini 1.5 Flash alongside the distributor's active product catalogue and schema constraints.
  3. Confidence Scoring: The model extracts product candidates and returns structured JSON with item confidence scores (0.0 to 1.0).
  4. Anti-Hallucination Guardrail: Items with confidence below 70% or SKU codes not present in the database are filtered out.
  5. Human-in-the-Loop Review: The extracted items are presented in an interactive review modal where the user can adjust quantities, select pack sizes, and confirm prices.

Offline & Timeout Fallback: If the Gemini API call exceeds 8 seconds or experiences rate limits, the backend automatically falls back to an internal FMCG regex and fuzzy matching parser (server/src/modules/ai/ai.service.ts). This extracts numbers, packaging keywords (peti, carton, darjan, kilo), and brand tokens locally without failing the user's order flow.

6. Offline-First Beat Salesman Engine

Salesmen in congested market sectors (Lordganj, Sarafa) regularly lose cellular connectivity. SupplySetu implements a reliable offline architecture using Dexie.js (IndexedDB):

  • Local Catalogue Caching: Product and retailer lists are cached locally in browser IndexedDB.
  • Idempotency Key Generation: Each offline order receives a cryptographically generated UUID v4 (clientOrderId) at the time of creation.
  • Transactional Store: Orders are written to Dexie's offlineOrders store with status PENDING_SYNC.
  • Automatic Synchronization: When connectivity is restored, a background listener submits queued orders to POST /api/orders/sync. The server checks the clientOrderId to prevent duplicate insertions, updates local records to SYNCED, and refreshes the salesman's order log.

7. UI & Design Decisions: Key Screens & UX Flow

  • Retailer Dashboard (RetailerDashboard.tsx): Mobile-first catalogue with live stock indicators, transparent retailer profit margin badges (e.g., "Your Margin: 18.6%"), 1-click reorder functionality, and a real-time khata balance meter.
  • AI Order Pad Modal (AiOrderPadModal.tsx): Split-view ingestion interface supporting text paste, speech dictation, or handwritten parchi image upload, followed by an interactive item verification table with confidence scores.
  • Beat Salesman Pad (SalesmanDashboard.tsx): Grouped by daily beat route sequences, displaying outstanding kirana balances before shop visits, quick order punch buttons, and an offline synchronization status badge.
  • Distributor Admin Order Desk (AdminDashboard.tsx): 12-column operational table with credit hold warnings, batch approval actions, streaming RFC-4180 Tally ERP CSV export, and an Executive Analytics suite featuring a 14-day kirana churn detection radar with one-click WhatsApp re-engagement links.

9. System Assumptions

  1. State GST Jurisdiction: Operations are localized to Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh (State Code 23), with standard 50/50 CGST/SGST splits on intra-state sales.
  2. Wholesale Packaging: Products are sold in commercial packaging units (BOX, CARTON, BAG). Loose individual pieces are not handled.
  3. Credit Terms: Retailers operate on rolling 15-day settlement cycles with credit ceilings enforced prior to order dispatch.
  4. Single-Distributor Tenancy: Designed for a single distributor entity serving multiple retail accounts across assigned geographical beats.
  5. Simulated OTP Sign-in: DLT registration delays are avoided by simulating mobile OTP verification with pre-configured demo credentials.
